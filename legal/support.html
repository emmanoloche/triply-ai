Frequently asked questions

How do I create a trip?

Tap Get started on the Home screen, tell us your destination, dates, budget, and travel style, and Triply's AI builds a full day-by-day itinerary in seconds.

How do I change a trip after it's generated?

Open the trip and use the assistant chat to ask for changes in plain language — for example "make day 2 cheaper" or "swap the museum for something outdoors." The itinerary updates to match.

Is the pricing and activity information accurate?

Itineraries, costs, and hotel suggestions are AI-generated estimates for planning purposes, not live data. Always confirm prices, availability, and booking details directly with the airline, hotel, or venue before you travel.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your trips, chat messages, and account data and can't be undone.

Something isn't working — what should I include in my message?

A quick description of what happened, the device and platform you're on (iOS, Android, or Web), and a screenshot if you have one. That helps us track down the issue faster.

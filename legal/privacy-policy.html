Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Triply (the "Service," "we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Triply mobile and web application. See our Terms of Service for the rules governing your use of the Service generally.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: your email address, name, and profile photo, obtained when you sign in with Google or Apple through our authentication provider, Clerk.
  • Trip information: the destinations, dates, traveler count, budget, pace, and interests you enter, plus the itinerary, budget breakdown, and hotel suggestions our AI generates for you.
  • Chat content: messages you send to the Assistant or to a trip's "Refine with AI" feature, and the AI's replies. Please don't include sensitive personal information (such as financial account numbers, government ID numbers, or health information) in these messages — we have no reason to collect it and don't ask for it.
  • Photos you upload: if you choose to replace a trip's cover photo with one from your own device.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: when an error occurs, or as part of routine app monitoring, we automatically collect crash reports, performance data, structured event logs (e.g. "trip generation requested"), and — for a sample of sessions — screen recordings with text, images, and input fields masked by default. Your account ID (not your email or name) is attached to this data so we can investigate issues tied to your account. This is collected via our error-monitoring provider, Sentry, and also includes standard technical data such as device/OS information and IP address as part of how that tooling normally operates.

We do not collect your device's location, contacts, or any sensor data — the app has no code that requests or uses these.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and maintain your account, and show you your own trips and conversations;
  • Generate itineraries, budgets, hotel suggestions, and chat replies by sending relevant trip and message details to our AI providers;
  • Find and display a representative cover photo for your trip's destination;
  • Diagnose and fix bugs, monitor app stability and performance, and understand feature usage at an aggregate, non-identifying level;
  • Enforce our usage limits (for example, the daily cap on trip generations) so the Service stays reliable for everyone.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your trip or chat content to serve you advertising — the Service has no advertising or ad-tracking code.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Triply, each receiving only what they need to perform their function:

  • Clerk — authentication and account identity.
  • OpenAI and Google Gemini — receive your trip preferences and chat messages as input to generate itineraries and replies. OpenAI is used first; Gemini is used automatically only if OpenAI is unavailable. Each provider processes this data under its own privacy policy and terms.
  • Unsplash — receives only the destination name (e.g. "Lisbon, Portugal") as a photo search query; no account or personal data is sent.
  • ImageKit — stores and serves trip cover photos, including any photo you upload yourself.
  • Neon — hosts our Postgres database, where your account, trip, and chat data is stored.
  • Sentry — receives the diagnostic and crash data described in Section 2.
  • Inngest — runs background jobs (like trip generation) and receives the data needed to carry those out.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Triply, our users, or others.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account, trip, and chat data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account (see Section 6), your trips, all Assistant and per-trip chat messages, and your usage records are permanently deleted immediately as part of that process; only a minimal record without your email, name, or photo is kept, solely to prevent a delayed system event from unintentionally restoring a deleted account.

Diagnostic data held by Sentry (crash reports, logs, session replays) is retained according to that provider's own retention settings, not a schedule we configure per record.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Delete your account: from the Profile screen, you can permanently delete your account and all associated trips and chat data. This is a real, immediate deletion, not a deactivation.
  • Clear your Assistant conversation: you can clear your saved Assistant chat history at any time from within the app.
  • Sign out: available at any time from the Profile screen.

We do not currently offer an automated "export your data" feature. If you'd like a copy of your data, contact us using the details in Section 9 and we'll do our best to help.

7. Children's Privacy

Triply is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 18 years old, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age. See our Terms of Service for our full eligibility requirement.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify you, such as an in-app notice. Continuing to use the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your data, can be sent to support@triply.com.
